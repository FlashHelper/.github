# FlashHelper

**Helping HCMUT students help each other.**

FlashHelper is a mobile application project developed by the **BaSauPhoPhuong** team for **CO3043 – Mobile Application Development** at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT).

Our goal is to explore how mobile technology can help students connect with nearby peers to request and offer assistance on campus.

## Project Overview

- **Target users:** HCMUT students
- **Project type:** Mobile application
- **Course:** CO3043 – Mobile Application Development
- **Team:** BaSauPhoPhuong

The project's direction, features, and scope are subject to research and validation throughout the product discovery process.

## Our Approach

We follow a user-centered product discovery process to understand student needs, investigate existing alternatives, validate critical assumptions, and define a feasible minimum viable product (MVP).

Our decisions will be guided by research evidence and documented as the project evolves.
